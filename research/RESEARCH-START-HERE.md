Retrieve old handoff, progress, reconciliation, branch, candidate, freeze, or release material when answering questions about:

- historical lineage or why a decision was made;
- released/candidate identity;
- high-consequence authority/recovery changes;
- an old failure/counterexample likely to be repeated;
- an independent-validation boundary where prior context would contaminate evidence;
- branch cleanup where unique unmerged material could be lost.
