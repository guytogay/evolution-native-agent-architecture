The clean v0.1 design uses:

- Sleep as reversible memory consolidation rather than summary generation;
- Dream as biased-random distant-memory recombination;
- recent/old/underused/external-or-unresolved/distant/salient/random sampling pools;
- associative-distance sampling when Host vectors are available, without universal fixed similarity thresholds;
- the owner's Divergent Explorer pattern as the main Dream variation operator;
- generated Dream material as speculative candidates rather than factual memory;
- existing Evolution/ACMS reality selection for what survives;
- feedback of selected positive, negative and null outcomes into later experience/consolidation.

Owner direction changed from design-only deferral to producing a usable first version for other Agents and improving it through field feedback. This permits bounded Sleep/Dream v0.1 field use while keeping the clean ENA product `ACTIVE_DEVELOPMENT / NOT_CURRENT / NOT_PROMOTED`.
