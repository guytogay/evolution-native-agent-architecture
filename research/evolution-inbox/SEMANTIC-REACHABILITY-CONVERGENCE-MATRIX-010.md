## What would justify changing adopter-facing Current without changing the Constitution

A `CURRENT_RETRIEVAL_CHANGE` is justified if fresh-instance tests repeatedly show:

1. exact Constitution semantics can support the right decision;
2. Current Runtime/Concept Map as-is reliably falls into a wrong attractor;
3. a compact cue, cross-family route, or example materially corrects the decision;
4. the repair transfers to lexically different cases;
5. the repair does not create worse false-BLOCK / false-ALLOW / governance burden.

This is a real Current defect even though no natural law is missing.

---

## What would justify a Constitution change

Require stronger evidence:

1. baseline fails;
2. cue/example/cross-family routing cannot resolve the decision without smuggling in a new normative relation;
3. two reasonable implementations of existing Current remain materially divergent;
4. the missing relation survives adversarial/cross-context testing;
5. adding it changes consequential decisions and does not merely make the prose more elegant.

Only then classify `TRUE_SEMANTIC_GAP`.
