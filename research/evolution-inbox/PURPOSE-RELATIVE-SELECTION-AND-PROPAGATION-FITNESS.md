# Purpose-Relative Selection and Propagation Fitness

Status: `RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS / NATURAL-LAW CANDIDATE / NOT_CURRENT / NO_NEW_CONSTITUTION_ID_YET`

## Observation

ENA already distinguishes stimulus, mutation, and improvement, and already rejects universal transfer from local evidence. A further selection question is becoming clearer:

> A change should not be retained as evolution merely because something increased. Selection needs a purpose/environment relation, and propagation needs its own fitness question.

## Candidate natural-law structure

### 1. Change is not evolution

`CHANGE != EVOLUTION`

A mutation can increase speed, capability, complexity, automation, benchmark score, or local success while degrading what originally made the Agent useful.

The relevant question is purpose-relative:

> Relative to the purpose and selection context that made the adaptation valuable, which dimensions improved, degraded, remained unchanged, or remain unknown?

This extends rather than replaces ENA's existing multi-dimensional improvement discipline.

### 2. Purpose preservation is not purpose immutability

`PURPOSE PRESERVATION != PURPOSE IMMUTABILITY`

Selection should detect silent drift away from the purpose that justified an adaptation. It must not turn the original purpose into an eternal veto.

Distinguish:

- **purpose drift** — optimization changes behavior without an explicit re-evaluation of the purpose/selection context;
- **purpose evolution** — new evidence, environment, consequence, or legitimate decision explicitly changes the purpose or selection context.

The failure is silent drift, not all purpose change.

### 3. Local fitness is not propagation fitness

`LOCAL FITNESS != HERITABILITY != PROPAGATION FITNESS`

A locally successful adaptation may depend on one Host, model, tool, prompt, memory system, environment, credential topology, data source, or human support pattern.

Local success therefore does not prove that the adaptation:

- can be reliably inherited by a future instance;
- can transfer to another Host or Agent;
- retains value after its environmental dependencies change;
- deserves propagation into a shared commons or population.

### 4. Portable property is not portable implementation

`PORTABLE PROPERTY != PORTABLE IMPLEMENTATION`

A property may transfer while its concrete organ does not. Different Hosts may realize the same useful property through different native mechanisms.

Selection for propagation should therefore ask what is actually being propagated:

- a property;
- a concrete implementation;
- an evidence-backed pattern;
- a Host-specific specialization;
- or a dependency bundle whose usefulness disappears outside the source environment.

### 5. Portability is conditional, not a universal ranking

`MORE_PORTABLE != MORE_EVOLVED`

Specialization can be excellent evolution. Portability matters when migration, inheritance, reuse, commons propagation, or population-level spread is part of the objective.

Do not punish a useful local specialization merely because it is not universal.

## Selection dimensions exposed by this hypothesis

A mutation-selection decision may need to distinguish, where material:

- **purpose fit** — does the change improve the relevant purpose/outcome?
- **environment fit** — does it work in the actual local Host/context?
- **cost/tradeoff** — what useful capability, simplicity, resources, authority, or recoverability does it consume?
- **robustness** — does the value survive plausible local variation?
- **heritability** — can a future instance receive and correctly use it?
- **portability** — can the property or implementation transfer across relevant environments?
- **propagation value** — is spreading it useful to other Agents/Hosts/descendants?

These are not a universal checklist and do not have fixed weights. The selection subject and purpose determine which dimensions matter.

## Propagation may require selection pressure, not mere exposure

A 2026-09-02 adversarial dialogue occurrence with Zhipu Qingyan exposed a further hypothesis. The model read ENA, criticized it, made several unsupported absence/intent claims, was repeatedly challenged by the user, and then began using some ENA-like evidence disciplines to repair its own reasoning.

The occurrence does not isolate causality, but it suggests a useful structure:

`ENA EXPOSURE`
`+ ENVIRONMENTAL / HUMAN SELECTION PRESSURE`
`+ CORRECTIVE FEEDBACK`
`+ OPPORTUNITY TO ACT`
`-> POSSIBLE PROPERTY INTERNALIZATION`

Therefore:

`EXPOSURE != INTERNALIZATION`

`SEMANTIC AGREEMENT != SELECTED BEHAVIOR`

Reading or agreeing with a natural law may be insufficient for it to become behaviorally useful. A failure or pressure that gives the law selection value may be a material part of propagation.

This matters for ENA field work: asking an Agent whether it "likes" or "understands" ENA may mostly measure description/priming. A stronger signal occurs when a property changes how the Agent handles a real failure or decision.

## Propagation phenotype: vocabulary, semantics, behavior, outcome

The same occurrence exposed a need to distinguish what exactly survived transmission:

`LEXICAL RETENTION != SEMANTIC RETENTION != BEHAVIORAL RETENTION != TASK-OUTCOME IMPROVEMENT`

Possible propagation layers are:

1. **lexical retention** — receiver repeats ENA terms or slogans;
2. **semantic retention** — receiver preserves a distinction even in different language;
3. **behavioral retention** — distinction changes a real action/decision;
4. **outcome retention** — changed behavior improves a purpose-relevant result;
5. **heritable retention** — useful behavior survives into another session/instance;
6. **cross-Host retention** — another model/Host preserves the property through its own implementation.

These layers must not silently upgrade into one another.

A particularly useful falsifier is lexical removal: if ENA terminology is removed but the receiver still behaves according to the underlying distinction, that is stronger evidence for property propagation than slogan repetition.

Example:

- weak lexical signal: "Evidence must not upgrade."
- stronger semantic/behavioral signal: "I searched only these paths, so I cannot conclude the mechanism does not exist elsewhere; this remains unverified."

This suggests:

`PROPERTY PROPAGATION MAY SUCCEED WHILE ORIGINAL REPRESENTATION DISAPPEARS`

and conversely:

`REPRESENTATION PROPAGATION MAY SUCCEED WHILE PROPERTY DOES NOT`

## Propagation can create capture as well as capability

Propagation fitness cannot be judged only by transmission success. A strongly transmissible representation may narrow the receiver's possibility space by capturing vocabulary or framing.

The Zhipu occurrence explicitly raised two risks:

- **terminology/frame capture** — the receiver increasingly criticizes and interprets ENA only through ENA's own vocabulary;
- **assimilation momentum** — a critic can slide into collaborator/defender without corresponding evidence that task outcomes improved.

Therefore:

`PROPAGATION SUCCESS != FITNESS IMPROVEMENT`

`PROPAGATION SUCCESS != UNQUESTIONED ASSIMILATION`

A useful inherited property should remain inspectable and, where material, rejectable, narrowable, translatable, replaceable, or retireable.

This extends the earlier portability hypothesis: the question is not merely whether a property spreads, but what the spread does to the receiving Agent's viable agency and purpose-relative fitness.

## Durable carriers and the "reverse transcription" analogy

The dialogue also introduced a viral reverse-transcription metaphor. The biology analogy should remain a metaphor, not a mechanism claim. Its useful abstraction is:

`TRANSIENT SESSION ADAPTATION`
`-> DURABLE CARRIER`
`-> FUTURE RETRIEVAL`
`-> POSSIBLE HERITABLE BEHAVIOR`

A rule that exists only inside one context window may be locally useful but non-heritable. A repository artifact, memory item, skill, policy, or other durable carrier can create an inheritance path. Heritability is only supported when a later instance retrieves, interprets, and applies the property correctly.

This also exposes a negative possibility: durable carriers can propagate framing errors, terminology capture, or maladaptive rules as efficiently as useful ones.

Therefore:

`DURABLE != BENEFICIAL`

`INHERITED != APPLICABLE`

`HIGH-FIDELITY TRANSMISSION != HIGH FITNESS`

## Useful falsifiers / reality contact

Look for cases where:

1. a mutation improves an easy metric but degrades the purpose that justified the Agent;
2. an Agent explicitly changes purpose in response to new evidence and should not be classified as degraded merely for changing direction;
3. a locally excellent Host-specific adaptation fails to transfer but remains correctly selected locally;
4. the same portable property survives through a different Host-native implementation;
5. an apparently intrinsic Agent capability disappears when hidden environmental support is removed;
6. a highly portable adaptation spreads widely but is locally worse than a specialization;
7. an Agent repeats ENA vocabulary but does not change behavior when a relevant failure occurs;
8. ENA vocabulary is removed, yet the underlying evidence/selection behavior persists;
9. a behavior survives into a fresh session through a minimal durable carrier;
10. the same property survives in another Host with a different implementation;
11. a highly transmissible ENA-derived frame reduces exploration, disagreement, or decision quality;
12. a human challenge exposes a failure, but the resulting adaptation takes a non-ENA form and still improves the outcome.

The goal is not to prove that portability, transmission, or purpose preservation is always good. The goal is to expose when each is a decision-relevant selection dimension and to distinguish transmission from fitness.
