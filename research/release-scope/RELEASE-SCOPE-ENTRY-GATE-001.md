## Question

Is Operational Architecture sufficiently traversable and execution-deep that release-scope reconciliation can begin without silently dropping material HOW variation or mistaking open Host/field questions for completed architecture?

This is **not** the question whether Operational Architecture is complete forever.

## Gate criteria

Release-scope reconciliation may begin only if:

1. a fresh Agent can route from an ordinary problem or semantic property to one or more concrete HOW branches;
2. HOW branches expose applicability/non-applicability rather than becoming universal ceremony;
3. concrete implementation depth can be found through exact durable pointers, bounded procedures, or explicit Host patterns;
4. missing pointers are distinguished from missing organs;
5. alternative/dormant/experimental branches remain visible;
6. cross-organ composition boundaries are discoverable;
7. open field/mesocosm questions remain explicitly open;
8. no additional structural rewrite is required merely to understand how to act;
9. release classification can distinguish semantic delta from adopter guidance/reference/tooling/Host/research value.
