# Lessons and Reminders

## Product lesson
Correct theory is not the product. A primary adopter surface must earn attention by changing useful action, evidence-seeking, selection, recovery, simplification or stop conditions.

`TRUE != USEFUL_PRODUCT_UNIT`

## Simplicity lesson
Do not solve complexity by adding a larger anti-complexity framework. Prefer deletion, compression, replacement and direct action.

Default check: `What is the simplest useful thing to do next?`

## Migration lesson
Do not copy v0.3.x structure into v0.4. Extract semantic value by decision consequence. Preserve history without preserving product debt.

## Machine-boundary lesson
Prefer machinery when machinery fits, but do not claim local validators can prove external authority, real-world effects, causal support, or future field behavior.

`MACHINE_FIRST_WHEN_MACHINE_FIT != MACHINE_ONLY`

## Research lesson
Do not reopen closed campaigns or manufacture experiments whose possible outputs cannot change the design decision.

## Human-work lesson
Do not use the owner as transport for work the Agent can execute. Human judgment and unavailable boundaries remain legitimate human roles.
