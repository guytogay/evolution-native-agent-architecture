# Recent Three Rounds

## Round 1 — Agency-first / Action Bridge diagnosis
The session recognized that ENA's Constitution is agency/evolution first, but the default hot projection is heavily defensive. F-208-13 was recorded. It also recognized that semantic distinctions such as `claim != evidence` or `migration != local validation` do not uniquely tell an Agent what to do next. F-208-14 was recorded and an Action Bridge concept developed.

## Round 2 — Practicality-first product critique
The owner sharpened the critique: ENA devotes large adopter-facing space to explaining theory and even proving obvious distinctions — compared to a book saying "cabbage != radish", "rain != flood", "Rome != Romania" while offering little practical guidance.

This produced F-208-15 and F-208-16 on `release/v0.3.15` and the candidate note `collaboration/reconciliation/2026-09-07-practicality-first-product-inversion.md`.

The session concluded this is not merely a wording defect but a product architecture defect: theory and derivation are in the primary product path, while useful action/HOW is secondary.

## Round 3 — Clean-root v0.4 decision + Issue #234
The owner questioned whether the large current ENA could be repaired at all because the root seemed wrong. The session chose not to refactor v0.3.x in place.

Created branch `rebuild/v0.4.0-practical-core` and two initial artifacts:
- `rebuild/v0.4.0/START-HERE.md`
- `rebuild/v0.4.0/PRACTICAL-CORE.md`

The rebuild treats v0.3.x as preserved research/semantic/provenance substrate and designs a practice-first successor from a blank product surface.

Issue #234 then appeared with a field-adopter "cabbage != radish" prose-rent critique. Its core observation strongly corroborates the product diagnosis, but its claim that a distinction only has value as a machine boundary was narrowed. Current ENFORCEMENT-MAP itself demonstrates that some important properties depend on external controls or field evidence.

The session also exposed the same complexity pathology in itself when it responded to a simple request for an anti-rigidity `AGENT.md` with an excessively long ruleset. The owner corrected this.
