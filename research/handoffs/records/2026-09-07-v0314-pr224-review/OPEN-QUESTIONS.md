# Open Questions

These are not preregistered experiments; they are the bounded product questions attached to PR #224.

1. Can the trigger-style direction be expressed in a compact form that preserves every existing durable distinction without adding defaults, mandatory procedures, magic aggregation rules, or false universality?
2. Does the compact trigger form materially improve salience/decision quality enough to justify extra resident context, or is it merely stylistically nicer?
3. Should the inequalities remain alongside trigger cues as compressed semantic checks, or can a refined trigger form replace them without losing precision?
4. Is any successor release justified after refinement, or should the contribution remain a design candidate until real use distinguishes the representations?

Do not answer these by preference alone. Use semantic review first; seek additional field evidence only if it can change the release decision.
