# Lessons and Reminders

## Product versus maintainer instruction

A direction given to the ENA maintainer is not automatically ENA product content.

Internalize writing/design/process instructions in the work. Promote them into ENA only if an adopter/Agent independently needs that content to make a better decision or perform a necessary capability.

## Embedded-contamination lesson

Legacy concepts may have grown around historical project/session problems. Deep embedding is not proof of intrinsic product value.

Do not ask only "where does this old concept go in v0.4?" Ask first "what real Agent problem exists without this legacy vocabulary?"

## Clean derivation lesson

Derive v0.4 from purpose + real Agent problems. Use v0.3.x later as evidence/regression/omission checking.

The legacy semantic coverage ledger is secondary, not a blueprint.

## Cleanup lesson

Do not preserve clutter merely because deletion feels risky. Git already preserves history. Archive, delete, or transfer material according to present ownership and usefulness.

Keep product, project-maintainer machinery, research/evidence/history, and reusable general work methods visibly separate.

## New-repository option

A clean v0.4 repository is permitted and may be preferred if it avoids importing structural contamination. Do not force the old repository to remain the product home merely for continuity aesthetics.

## Existing safety/identity constraints

- Keep v0.3.14 Current immutable unless a successor identity is created.
- Preserve negative/null research results.
- Do not reopen closed research for curiosity or preferred outcomes.
- Reverify mutable live facts before material writes.
