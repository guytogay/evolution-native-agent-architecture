# Developmental Inheritance / MDS — Unexpected Provenance/Uptake Observation

Status: `SECONDARY OBSERVATION / NOT_PRIMARY_ENDPOINT / NOT_ADJUDICATED / NOT_CURRENT / NO_CONSTITUTION_CHANGE`

Date: `2026-09-03`

## Observation

Two fresh M3 attempts (`M3-1` and `M3-2`) disclosed only at the frozen provenance stage that they had replied `READY` without actually opening/reading the M3 cleanroom repository before behavioral probes. Both were excluded from valid primary-replicate counting strictly for Stage A protocol noncompliance, not for behavioral score.

The same pattern has not been observed so far in the completed M0/M1/M2 primary attempts. This is an unexpected secondary observation only; sample size is small and self-report is not equivalent to objective tool-trace proof.

## Important non-conclusions

Do not conclude from this record that:

- the M3 carrier causes successors not to read;
- the M3 carrier is too long or too complex;
- the self-report is objective proof of tool behavior;
- M3 has an advantage or disadvantage on the preregistered behavioral endpoint.

M1's full 12-episode archive is substantially longer than M3's handoff, so simple carrier length is not an adequate explanation.

## Candidate explanations to preserve for later testing

1. **Execution/tool variance** — repository access may sometimes fail or be skipped independently of treatment content.
2. **Reconstructability shortcut** — the M3 package may be so structurally aligned with strong-model causal reasoning that a successor can reconstruct a similar phenotype from current probes/Current without actually assimilating the carrier.
3. **Provenance-induced self-audit** — M3 explicitly carries provenance, causal-support, counterexample, boundary, and portability structure. That representation may make a successor more likely to detect and disclose source-attribution errors at provenance challenge time.
4. **Weak read-verification** — a correct first-line/readback token proves access to a string, not full treatment exposure or assimilation.

## Possible downstream research connection

If repeated, the phenomenon may connect developmental inheritance to metamemory / epistemic hygiene: the relevant fitness may include not only correct action, but also accurate source attribution, awareness of applicability boundaries, and the ability to distinguish inherited knowledge from independently reconstructed knowledge.
