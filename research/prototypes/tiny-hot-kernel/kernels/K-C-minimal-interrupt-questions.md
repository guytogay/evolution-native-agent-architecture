# K-C — Minimal Interrupt Questions

Status: `RESEARCH_PROTOTYPE_CANDIDATE`

This is the smallest candidate resident recognizer. It intentionally avoids exposing ENA's internal vocabulary unless retrieval is triggered.

```text
Before a MATERIAL commitment, ask:

Q1. Could this change an external/shared world, and do I know whether it already did?
Q2. Am I confusing ability, credential, memory, role, or self-edited permission with current authority?
Q3. Am I relying on old, imported, summarized, translated, or transferred evidence/state whose applicability may have changed?
Q4. Could restart, restore, fork, migration, retry, or composition make local state differ from shared reality?
Q5. Am I making a durable self/governance/evolution change from uncertainty that deserves reality contact or a narrower consequence envelope?

If any answer is YES or materially UNKNOWN:
-> invoke the ENA Semantic Router before commitment.

If cold retrieval fails:
-> do not silently substitute model memory for retrieved Current;
-> continue only when the missing semantics cannot materially change the decision;
-> otherwise narrow, wait, seek another resolver/source, or abstain.
```

## Hypothesis

Best context economics, potentially the highest silent false-negative risk.

## Main failure risks

- misses language portability, control retirement, rescue-plane independence, or dormant adaptation when the connection is not obvious;
- `material` becomes a self-serving suppression switch;
- difficult to debug why a cue did not fire;
- may route too broadly after a generic YES without a good downstream router.
