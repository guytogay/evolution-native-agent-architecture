# Anti-Convergence Checklist

Canonical detailed method:

`CONVERGENCE-DIVERGENCE-DISCIPLINE.md`

Fast rule:

```text
COMPRESS REPRESENTATION != COMPRESS POSSIBILITY SPACE
```

Before reducing any ENA research surface, require a disposition for every materially distinct predecessor variation:

```text
PRESERVED
MERGED_AS_PROVEN_EQUIVALENT
REPLACED_BY_STRONGER_ORACLE_OR_MECHANISM
RETAINED_AS_DORMANT
RETIRED_WITH_EVIDENCE
LOST
UNKNOWN
```

Do not call `LOST` or `UNKNOWN` successful simplification.
