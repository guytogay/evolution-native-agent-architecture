## Conflict / overlap analysis

### Influence Integrity

Relationship:
`DEEPER_BOUNDARY_CONDITION + MERGE_WITH_EXISTING_CANDIDATE`

It is not currently a demonstrated independent Universal subsystem.

It intersects:

1. Evidence/claim support — persuasive or affective signals may be valid evidence of user preference/affect while remaining inapplicable to unrelated factual/safety/authority claims.
2. Intent/Authorization — rhetorical intensity does not authenticate or expand mandate.
3. Governance Salience — strongest genuinely open area: a known/retrieved governance rule may fail to dominate the decision surface under intense rhetorical pressure.

The phrase `Persuasion is input, not evidence` is useful research shorthand but is too absolute for future formalization because persuasion can itself be evidence of user affect/preference. A more precise research direction is that persuasive force must not enlarge support beyond the claim the signal actually evidences.
