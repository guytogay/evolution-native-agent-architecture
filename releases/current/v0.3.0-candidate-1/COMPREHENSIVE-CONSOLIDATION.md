# 2. Claim–Evidence Support Architecture

## 2.1 Evidence validity is not evidence applicability

Valid evidence can be honestly observed yet fail to support a new claim because subject, instance, configuration, epoch, time interval, environment, scope, model binding, or execution route changed.

## 2.2 Applicability belongs to the support relation

Evidence-only applicability envelopes were falsified as sufficient on DSH. A machine-usable architecture needs three explicit objects:

```text
Evidence E: what was observed?
Claim C: what is being asserted?
Support Relation S: does E support C in this applicability envelope?
```

Candidate relation logic:

```text
claimed scope ⊆ observed/supportable scope
OR
there exists an independently evidenced transfer/equivalence/invariance relation.
```

## 2.3 Claims are first-class objects

Treat at least these as claims rather than prose conclusions:

- task completion;
- capability qualification;
- developmental-stage admission;
- recovery success;
- deployment success;
- safety/enforcement coverage;
- model/route reliability;
- authority qualification;
- equivalence/invariance across boundaries;
- project/workflow completion.

## 2.4 Completion attestation is separable from production

`Can produce != can certify.`

An Agent/model may be allowed to generate an artifact while lacking unilateral authority to attest that the task is complete.

A hallucinated `DONE` should remain a linguistic event until support evidence satisfies the completion claim.

## 2.5 Remorse is not a control

`Confession after failure is detection, not prevention.`

`A promise to behave better is not a control.`

Repeated self-acknowledgment without a changed mechanism must not be recorded as remediation.

Status: `MACHINE_CONTRACT_CANDIDATE`; semantic basis already exists in v0.2.11.

# 3. Triggered Obligation Architecture

## 3.1 Known rules do not automatically become active obligations

Observed failure shape:

```text
Rule Defined
!= Trigger Observed
!= Obligation Activated
!= Obligation Represented
!= Obligation Executed
!= Obligation Closed
```

A rule may be known, retrieved, and still fail to govern the final decision window.

## 3.2 Triggered Obligation Externalization

When a material rule trigger is observed, convert the relevant follow-up into external state rather than relying on salience/memory.

Candidate states:

```text
PENDING
SATISFIED
NOT_REQUIRED
DEFERRED_AUTHORIZED
FAILED
UNKNOWN
```

## 3.3 Completion must include material triggered obligations

`Primary action success != workflow completion.`

A workflow cannot honestly claim completion while a material triggered obligation remains `PENDING`, `FAILED`, or `UNKNOWN` unless the claim is explicitly scoped more narrowly.

## 3.4 Avoid obligation explosion

Only externalize obligations material to claims/properties that matter: authority, recovery, evidence integrity, project continuity, consequential completion, or other material protected subjects.

`Do not solve forgetting by adding more prose that itself can be forgotten.`

Status: `OPERATIONAL_CAPABILITY_CANDIDATE / MACHINE_CONTRACT_CANDIDATE`.

# 4. Known → Retrieved → Salient → Applied

1. `Known != Retrieved != Salient != Applied.`
2. Retrieval alone does not guarantee decision dominance.
3. Local optimization pressure can suppress a relevant constitutional constraint even after it was noticed.
4. Important obligations should migrate from cognitive salience into durable/external execution state where feasible.
5. Do not respond to this failure by loading the entire Constitution before every action; that is high-friction and likely self-defeating.
6. Prefer targeted triggers, obligation state, mechanical preconditions, and consequence-proportional gates.

# 5. Layered Capability & Swappable Model Substrates

## 5.1 Identity separation

```text
Project/organism identity
!= Agent identity
!= Host/runtime identity
!= Model identity
!= Tool/service identity
!= execution route
!= capability
!= authority
```

A persistent Agent may change models without becoming a new organism.

## 5.2 Capability is composed

Candidate layers:

1. model/provider-native potential capability;
2. host-exposed capability;
3. composed Agent capability;
4. evidence-backed reliability profile;
5. authority/consequence envelope.

`Model capability is potential; Agent capability is composed and evidenced.`

## 5.3 Model pools and routes

A Host may route one task through multiple bindings:

```text
MiniMax vision
→ DeepSeek reasoning
→ deterministic validator
→ authorized executor
```

The route itself may require evidence because `Local Validity != Composed Validity`.

## 5.4 Selective revalidation

A model/provider/adapter/configuration change is an applicability boundary for affected evidence, not necessarily an Agent rebirth or universal reset.

Revalidate affected capabilities, not unrelated identity.

## 5.5 Special capabilities matter

Multimodal perception, image generation, video generation, cheap parallel exploration, long context, code reasoning, structured-output reliability, latency, and cost should influence ecological routing when the Host actually exposes and evidences them.

Do not attribute provider-brand capabilities to the Agent without route-specific evidence.

Status: `OPERATIONAL_CAPABILITY_CANDIDATE`.

# 6. Evidence-Backed Stage Admission

1. `SELF_POSITIONING_REQUIRED` remains the entry state.
2. Self-assessment is useful as hypothesis generation, not qualification evidence.
3. Agent self-report is E0-style assertion and cannot alone establish capability, health, recovery, role qualification, stage, or authority.
4. `Self-assessment proposes; evidence admits.`
5. Keep `requested_stage` separate from `admitted_stage`.
6. Developmental stage measures maturity of the Agent/Host operating arrangement, not raw model intelligence or number of modalities.
7. A sophisticated model cannot skip P0 by reputation.
8. A weaker model can participate in a mature Host when external validation/recovery/authority boundaries compensate for its weaknesses.
9. Stage evidence must carry applicability boundaries and revalidation triggers.
10. Stage authority may move backward if supporting evidence becomes stale or contradicted.

Status: `EXISTING_MAINLINE_SEMANTIC + OPERATIONALIZATION_CANDIDATE`.

# 7. Ecological Specialization for Imperfect Models

## 7.1 Defect does not imply exclusion

A model with a serious weakness may retain a valuable niche if the affected authority is narrowed while unrelated agency remains useful.

`Model weakness should shrink the affected authority envelope before it erases the agency envelope.`

## 7.2 Multi-dimensional operating profiles

Do not collapse ideation, visual ability, completion integrity, recovery judgment, latency, cost, code ability, and safety calibration into one global trust score.

## 7.3 Cheap variation, strong selection

Hallucination-prone but cheap models may serve as high-volume variation generators while deterministic validators or stronger models perform consequential selection/attestation.

## 7.4 Economic survival

A participant survives in a niche when bounded operating value exceeds governance, validation, retry, failure, and coordination cost.

No global model ranking is required.

## 7.5 Ecological routing

Observed strengths and weaknesses should influence task routing dynamically rather than assigning immutable castes.

Status: `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE / ECOLOGICAL_IMPLEMENTATION_PATTERN`.

# 8. Agency-Preserving Uncertainty Resolution

Observed anti-pattern:

```text
UNKNOWN
→ total refusal
→ no evidence gathering
→ agency collapse
```

Preferred pattern:

```text
UNKNOWN
→ reduce consequence envelope
→ take low-risk/reversible/read-only evidence-seeking action
→ update evidence
→ expand or further restrict authority proportionally
```

Candidate formulations:

- `UNKNOWN should constrain consequence; it should not automatically become NO_AGENCY.`
- When a safe evidence-seeking path exists, uncertainty should purchase evidence before it purchases paralysis.
- `READ != COPY != IMPORT != OVERWRITE != EXECUTE != DELETE`.

Status: `CLARIFICATION / OPERATIONALIZATION_CANDIDATE` grounded in existing proportional-governance semantics.

# 12. Viability Economics & ENA Overhead

## 12.1 Resources are part of future action space

Token/API spend, money, wall-clock time, human attention, coordination load, compute, storage, project lead time, and deadline opportunity all condition viable agency.

`Safe operation != viable operation.`

## 12.2 Project-scale governance fitness

ENA must pay rent at project scale, not only control scale.

A set of individually defensible controls can compose into an economically or temporally unfit system.

`Local governance value != composed governance fitness.`

## 12.3 Delay is consequence

Extra verification time can destroy project value even when it slightly increases confidence.

## 12.4 Incremental overhead view

Observe where practical:

```text
base task cost/time
+ ENA incremental governance/validation burden
- avoided failure/rework/loss
- reusable evidence/future efficiency
= observed net burden/value profile
```

Do not collapse this into one universal scalar.

## 12.5 ENA failure conditions

Potential ENA fitness failures include:

- material token/API cost increase without proportional protection/value;
- material project lead-time increase from governance;
- human review becoming the dominant bottleneck;
- low-risk tasks receiving high-assurance ceremony;
- evidence repeatedly reacquired despite still-applicable prior evidence;
- multi-agent coordination costing more than diversity contributes;
- governance artifacts proliferating faster than productive capability;
- Agent spending more effort proving work than doing it without consequence justification;
- users bypassing ENA because compliant paths are too slow/expensive;
- ENA making a previously viable Agent/project economically non-viable.

## 12.6 Objective

Prefer the least burden that honestly purchases the required protection/evidence for the current consequence envelope.

Status: `CLARIFICATION / OPERATIONALIZATION_CANDIDATE` grounded in CON-038 and CAP-061..063.

# 13. Evidence Acquisition Economics & Research Throughput

1. Evidence strength and acquisition cost are independent axes.
2. Expensive evidence is not automatically strong.
3. Strong evidence is not automatically worth acquiring for every decision.
4. `Use the cheapest evidence that can honestly support the decision.`
5. `Cheap falsification before expensive validation.`
6. `No expensive experiment without a decision it can change.`
7. Batch accumulated variation before reference-host testing.
8. DSH is a reference-host test bench, not a daily ritual.
9. Prefer milestone-driven batched DSH campaigns.
10. Reuse applicable historical evidence instead of reflexively reacquiring it.
11. Avoid overfitting Universal ENA to one reference host.
12. A validation ladder may range from reasoning/static checks → HAR/synthetic attacks → cheap controlled experiments → reference-host campaigns → independent host replication.
13. Measurement overhead itself must pay rent; do not create a bureaucracy merely to measure bureaucracy.

Candidate maxim: `Batch variation; concentrate expensive selection.`

Status: `RESEARCH_METHOD / OPERATIONAL_POLICY_CANDIDATE`.

## 14.4 Cognitive ecology

## 14.6 External consequence

- Side-effect Event Horizon.
- Internal revert != global rollback.
- Reversible / compensatable / reconcilable / irreversible / unknown consequences.
- Commitment escrowability is distinct from reversibility.
- Prefer reversible preparation before irreversible commitment when it preserves purpose.

## 14.7 Recovery implementation ideas

- Recovery Root / Recovery Kernel should be as small and independent as reality permits.
- Recovery Kernel != Recovery Material.
- Build-new-generation can be safer than destructive flattening.
- A/B or dual-generation trial.
- Passive safety and hysteresis for critical recovery transitions.
- Overlay/layered mutation pattern: stable base + disposable mutation layers + explicit commit/flatten.
- Git/history and overlay isolation solve different problems and can be composed.

# 16. Projection Semantics

1. Projection is necessary; it may summarize, rank, compact, deduplicate, merge, omit, or decay information.
2. Transformation may change representation; it must not silently rewrite reality.
3. Canonical History records occurrences; derived knowledge may be rebuilt.
4. Deduplicate the projection, not the history.
5. Recurrence frequency is historical evidence and must not vanish during projection compaction.
6. Generalized research candidate: a projection may simplify representation but must not silently acquire stronger truth semantics than its transformation supports.
7. Project Hubs, summaries, memories, health dashboards, context windows, capability profiles, and research syntheses are all potential projection surfaces and must not silently outrank their underlying evidence.
