## Required interpretation

- `v0.2.11 MAINLINE` is still the formal baseline.
- Candidate 1 and Candidate 2 are not promoted Mainline.
- Candidate 1 is the broad research/consolidation map.
- Candidate 2 is the productionization and living-evolution increment.
- Learning a candidate does not authorize implementing every candidate mechanism.
- Using ENA does not grant authority to promote ENA changes.
- Local field experience is contribution evidence, not automatic Universal truth.

## For production adopters

Start with the declared production profile, not the entire research surface.

Use the broader candidate documents when a relevant failure occurs, a mechanism is being evaluated, field evidence is being contributed, or reconciliation/research is explicitly requested.
