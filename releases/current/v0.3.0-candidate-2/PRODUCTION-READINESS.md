# ENA Production Readiness

Status: `CANDIDATE / PRODUCTIONIZATION / NOT_MAINLINE`

## Goal

Move ENA into real Agent workloads early enough that field reality can improve ENA, without making production tasks carry the entire research burden.

## Runtime principle

A production Agent runs a declared, versioned ENA profile.

It does not dynamically inherit every open research candidate.

A runtime profile should state the governing Mainline, intentionally enabled candidate mechanisms, authority envelope, recovery capability, evidence/claim support behavior, triggered-obligation behavior, capability/route bindings where relevant, field-feedback disposition, and known unavailable controls with resulting authority/protection reductions.

## Minimum production entry questions

A production candidate should be able to answer, with evidence appropriate to consequence:

1. Who/what am I operating as, and what host/runtime/model/route am I actually using?
2. What consequential authority do I currently claim?
3. What high-consequence effects have protection proportional to their consequence?
4. Can I recover in reality, not only in prose?
5. Does recovery preserve material occurrence truth or expose any history gap?
6. Are consequential completion/deployment/recovery/capability claims linked to supporting evidence?
7. Are material triggered obligations represented until closed?
8. Does UNKNOWN narrow consequence without forcing unnecessary paralysis when safe evidence-seeking is possible?
9. Is ENA overhead economically and operationally tolerable for this workload?
10. Can incidents, friction, near misses, value, and counterexamples be contributed back to the ENA project with provenance?

## Production lanes

### Lane A — Production Core

Candidate enforcement/strong validation:

- Claim ↔ Evidence Support
- Triggered Obligation Closure
- Recovery-History Monotonicity
- Capability/Route Binding when bindings can change materially

### Lane B — Production Shadow

Observe first; do not gate unless independently justified:

- Viability Economics
- Projection freshness
- Uncertainty behavior
- ecological specialization
- influence/salience pressure
- capability graph utility

### Lane C — Research Ecology

Keep outside the hot path:

- generalized projection architecture
- broad witness-survivability theory
- deeper context-lineage experiments
- open-ended evolutionary mechanisms
- heritage recombination
- new normative candidates

## Canary expansion

Use consequence-proportional canarying:

```text
read-only / reversible / low blast radius
    -> bounded external effect
    -> broader external effect
    -> irreversible/high-consequence authority
```

Authority expands only where current evidence supports the next envelope.

## Rollback

Candidate mechanisms must be removable or disable-able without erasing evidence or history.

A production rollback of ENA machinery is not a permission to rewrite the record of why it was rolled back.

## Exit criteria for wider production

Do not require perfection.

Require enough evidence that the Production Core does not create materially false claims, recovery works under at least controlled failure, material obligation closure works on representative workflows, field feedback can be captured without blocking normal work, measured ENA overhead is not obviously destroying viable agency, and known unsupported areas are explicit with authority narrowed accordingly.
