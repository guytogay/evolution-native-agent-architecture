# CONTRIBUTION PROTOCOL — v0.3.5 candidate

All legitimate ENA participants may read project knowledge, critique, propose variations, experiment within actual authority/consequence boundaries, and contribute evidence/adaptations.

Recommended contribution classes:

`INCIDENT`, `NEAR_MISS`, `FRICTION`, `VALUE_OBSERVED`, `COUNTEREXAMPLE`, `PORTABILITY_FINDING`, `NEW_VARIATION`, `ADAPTATION_PACKET`, `EMERGENCE_FINDING`, `EVIDENCE_RESULT`, `RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS`, `CRITIQUE`.

When useful include:

- ENA candidate/release identity;
- Host/runtime/model/language/configuration;
- Evolutionary Subject / Protected Subject(s);
- Variation Space;
- event/outcome;
- improved/degraded/unknown dimensions;
- migration applicability;
- evidence/provenance;
- uncertainty and alternative explanations.

Contribution does not grant reconciliation, release/Current promotion, deployment, or unrelated consequential authority.

A contributor may challenge the current selection without first proving a replacement is better. Variation generation and stable admission are different functions.
