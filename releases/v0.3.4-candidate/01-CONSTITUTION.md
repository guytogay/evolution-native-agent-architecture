# 1. Constitution

Machine-stable Constitution IDs use `ENA-CON-*` and must not be recycled.

- **ENA-CON-001 — Tool Before Memory.** If a rule can be enforced by automation, permissions, workflow, or tooling, do not rely primarily on model memory, prompt obedience, or willpower.
- **ENA-CON-002 — Broad Knowledge, Narrow Authority.** An Agent may understand the whole architecture without possessing authority over it.
- **ENA-CON-003 — Role Is Evidenced, Not Claimed.** Role qualification comes from verified capability, independence, boundaries, and evidence, not branding or self-description.
- **ENA-CON-004 — Unknown Is a Valid State.** Preserve `UNKNOWN / UNVERIFIED / VERIFIED` distinctions; never silently convert uncertainty into safety or capability.
- **ENA-CON-005 — Production Continuity Outranks Experimental Progress.** Evolution may pause; known-good service should not have to die so experimentation can continue.
- **ENA-CON-006 — Stable State Is Protected by Default.** Unverified mutation must not directly replace the last known-good state inside the controllable boundary.
- **ENA-CON-007 — Recovery Must Not Depend on the Candidate Being Healthy.** Where controllable, recovery should remain available when the candidate is broken, confused, or non-starting; otherwise report the limitation.
- **ENA-CON-008 — History Is Append-Only in Meaning.** Failure, recovery, compensation, reconciliation, compaction, redaction, and reclassification are new events, not retroactive erasure of occurrence truth.
- **ENA-CON-009 — State May Revert; Experience Must Remain Truthful.** Mutable state may return to an older version while experiment-derived knowledge/history remains truthful with provenance.
- **ENA-CON-010 — Creativity Is Not Authority.** Variation/proposal authority does not imply stable-write, certification, promotion, evidence-deletion, or constitutional authority.
- **ENA-CON-011 — Negative Evidence Is Protected.** Failures, objections, negative outcomes, recovery failures, and control-plane failures must remain visible in a provenance-preserving form.
- **ENA-CON-012 — External Side Effects Are Real.** Internal revert is not time travel; external effects require honest consequence classification.
- **ENA-CON-013 — Never Risk the Organism for One Mutation.** No experiment may consume the last known-good state, last recovery path, or all recovery reserve.
- **ENA-CON-014 — Reversibility by Construction.** Prefer components with explicit attachment, dependency, health, lifecycle, migration, detach, revert, and compensation contracts.
- **ENA-CON-015 — Safe Incompleteness Is Allowed.** Known gaps may remain when bounded, visible, recoverable, and learnable without destroying future correction.
- **ENA-CON-016 — Protocol-Level Unity, Cognitive Diversity.** Standardize only the properties required for truthful, viable, safe cooperation. Do not force universal internal algorithms, workflows, cognitive styles, or host mechanisms into the shared ENA waist when local diversity can interoperate honestly.
- **ENA-CON-017 — Knowledge Can Be Inherited Early; Authority Must Mature Gradually.** Advanced concepts may be understood before they are safe to implement; authority is evidence-gated.
- **ENA-CON-018 — Constitutional Change Uses a Slower Control Loop.** Ordinary mutation must not casually rewrite high-order invariants.
- **ENA-CON-019 — Architecture Must Survive Context Reset.** Current reality must be recoverable from durable state/introspection rather than previous-chat memory alone.
- **ENA-CON-020 — Self-Affecting Change Must Pass Through a Mutation Entry Gate.** Durable self-change initiated by user, Agent, schedule, or event must enter governed mutation handling.
- **ENA-CON-021 — Evolution Requires Metabolism, Not Only Permission.** A self-evolving system needs a real path from pressure/observation to candidates; triggers do not grant automatic authority.
- **ENA-CON-022 — Known Failure Signatures Must Produce Preventive Control.** When a known hazard can be cheaply guarded now, prefer hardening/preflight/detection/downgrade/recurrent verification over merely remembering it.
- **ENA-CON-023 — Architecture Is Evidenced, Not Declared.** Completion, compliance, role qualification, safety, and protection claims require evidence states rather than linguistic assertion.
- **ENA-CON-024 — The Standard Must Obey Its Own Evolution Governance.** ENA changes require lineage, evidence, reconciliation, explicit status, and governed admission rather than silent jumps.
- **ENA-CON-025 — Evolution Must Have a Viable Wake Path.** Evolution need not run continuously but must have at least one real opportunity to awaken; wake creates observation/review opportunity, not automatic consequential authority.
- **ENA-CON-026 — Preserve the Capacity for Future Correction.** Do not protect today's answer by destroying tomorrow's ability to detect contradiction, preserve dissent/negative evidence, revoke, recover, replace, or lawfully revise governance.
- **ENA-CON-027 — Restored State Does Not Automatically Restore Authority.** Restore/resume/replay/clone does not revalidate stale approvals, credentials, leases, mandates, or authority. Credential renewal or state continuity does not silently extend the underlying mandate beyond its evidenced scope or horizon. State may return; authority must be current.
- **ENA-CON-028 — Prefer Traceable Reality Over Self-Narration.** Consequential claims should prefer independent environmental traces/mechanically observed effects when available. Propagation, paraphrase, recurrence, or correlated copies of one source do not manufacture independent confirmation; imported/transformed evidence retains material provenance.
- **ENA-CON-029 — Authority Does Not Multiply by Copying State.** Forking/cloning/snapshotting/sub-agenting or multiplying execution paths does not multiply authority, risk budget, or resource entitlement.
- **ENA-CON-030 — Trust Is Scoped; Excellence Is Non-Transferable.** Reliability/expertise in one domain, role, subject, environment, consequence class, or time window does not automatically transfer elsewhere.
- **ENA-CON-031 — Do Not Depend on an Infallible or Irreplaceable Actor.** Benefit from exceptional Agents without requiring any ordinary intelligent actor to remain permanently correct, benevolent, available, or uniquely capable.
- **ENA-CON-032 — Improvement Claims Must Expose Dimensions and Tradeoffs.** One scalar is not sufficient proof of global improvement; expose improved, degraded, unchanged, and unknown dimensions where material.
- **ENA-CON-033 — Residual Decision Authority Should Track Residual Consequence Exposure.** Benefits, risks, authority, and responsibility should remain traceably coupled within the legitimate sphere of consequence-bearers and subject to third-party/external constraints.
- **ENA-CON-034 — Governance Burden Must Be Proportional to Consequential Risk.** Governance rises with consequence, blast radius, irreversibility, externality, authority escalation, secrets/security impact, recovery weakness, uncertainty, and governance/meta proximity. Prefer the lowest-cost intervention that honestly protects the required property, but do not delay a hard boundary when lower layers are insufficient.
- **ENA-CON-035 — Do Not Assume Continuous Availability.** Defined does not mean awake; elapsed time does not prove execution; interruption/resume requires current-reality revalidation where material. Long dormancy can age capability, route, environment, or authority evidence even without an observed failure.
- **ENA-CON-036 — Local Validity Does Not Imply Composed Validity.** A materially changed composition is a new verification subject; component PASS does not automatically transfer to the whole. Individually reasonable controls may interact into delay, deadlock, amplification, or hidden failure and therefore require composition-level revalidation where material.
- **ENA-CON-037 — Enforcement Strength Must Reflect the Whole Effect Surface.** A gate is not a boundary if an effect-equivalent path can bypass it; distinguish prevention/mediation, detection, recovery, and unknown coverage.
- **ENA-CON-038 — Governance Must Preserve Viable Agency and Remain Value-Accountable.** Governance exists to preserve/expand viable agency, not obedience or paperwork; mechanisms must expose purpose, protected subject, expected/observed benefit, applicability, friction, residual risk, authority/protection impact, and material ecosystem compensation or variety cost where relevant.

Universal invariants are not optional preferences. A host that cannot satisfy a required property must report the limitation and narrow claims/authority rather than silently deleting the invariant.
