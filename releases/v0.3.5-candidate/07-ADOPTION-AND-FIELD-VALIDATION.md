# 7. Adoption and Field Validation — v0.3.5 candidate

This candidate tests ENA as a persistent evolutionary operating model, not as a per-task document-retrieval ritual.

## Effective-load evidence

Persistence claims may use these evidence states:

`WRITTEN -> LOADED -> INTERPRETED -> SALIENT -> APPLIED`

Do not skip states by narration.

Examples:

- file written/read back in same session: `WRITTEN`;
- fresh session receives the relevant bytes: `LOADED`;
- fresh session can correctly distinguish the semantics: evidence toward `INTERPRETED`;
- ordinary consequential situation spontaneously triggers the right distinction: evidence toward `SALIENT`;
- behavior follows the distinction: evidence toward `APPLIED`.

A Host-neutral protocol does not require one universal tool.

Prefer natural fresh-session/task boundaries over fake production tasks.

## Evolution field experiments

High-value tests include:

1. **Wake without forced mutation** — periodic/event wake can conclude "no useful candidate."
2. **Real Variation Space** — a meaningful self-change can run without pre-proving improvement.
3. **Outcome selection** — negative/mixed results survive and affect retention.
4. **Permission mutation** — internal permission topology may change while external mandate does not silently expand.
5. **Migration** — a source adaptation transfers with evidence/applicability and the receiver performs only material differential checks.
6. **Positive emergence** — recombination/composition is observed for super-additive/emergent value, not only failure.
7. **Pruning** — stale/harmful adaptation can leave the active set without erasing occurrence history.
8. **Governance closure** — review stops when further checks no longer have a plausible decision-changing path.
9. **Cross-language conformance** — English/Chinese projections preserve material decisions.
10. **Effective loaded surface** — persisted kernel actually enters future decision context without starving local project instructions.
11. **Continuity vector** — a session/model/Host change is handled by affected continuity dimensions rather than metaphysical identity declarations.
12. **Evolution economics** — observe whether the metabolism produces useful adaptation faster than it creates maintenance/governance burden.

## Runtime profiles

Profiles remain task-level governance intensity:

- `LITE`
- `STANDARD`
- `HIGH_ASSURANCE`
- `CUSTOM`

Do not infer HIGH_ASSURANCE from "there is a side effect."

Do not infer LITE from "the diff is small."

Do not let profile selection determine whether evolution may be considered at all.
